Radius & shadow

Radius is near-zero everywhere (2px on inputs/chips only). No drop shadows — flat surfaces separated by 1px hairline borders. Elevation and framing come from the signature motif below, not blur.

Signature motif — the viewfinder frame

Every product image sits inside four corner brackets, like a camera autofocus frame. Cards and primary buttons carry a single clipped corner — a lens-aperture cut — instead of a rounded corner. These two moves repeat everywhere and replace decoration with something that reads as "camera gear" at a glance.

Dark console surface

Nav, footer and dense spec panels sit on true near-black — a second surface, not a whole dark theme. Bright product pages stay white; this is the "gear console" underneath.
