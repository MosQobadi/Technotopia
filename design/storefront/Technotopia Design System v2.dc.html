Radius, shadow & the record-dot motif

Buttons are full pills. Cards and product trays are large-radius flat gray, never bordered, never shadowed — depth comes from the gray-on-white fill, not elevation. A small filled dot — a tally-light — sits before every eyebrow label and section tag: the one recurring mark that reads "recording gear" without drawing an icon.
